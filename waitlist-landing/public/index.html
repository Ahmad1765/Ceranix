What is Grabsty and how does the waitlist work? +

Grabsty is a disciplined marketplace for curated archival fashion and Lahore Architecture Design. Joining the waitlist reserves your priority access when our marketplace opens in Lahore. You will be among the first to explore listings, buy, and sell with escrow protection.

How does the Escrow Buyer Protection safeguard purchases? +

When you purchase an item on Grabsty, your funds are safely held in escrow — they are never released directly to the seller upon checkout. The seller ships with tracked courier transit. Upon delivery, you have a full 72-hour physical inspection window to verify that the piece precisely matches the listing before payment is released.

Why do you require a phone number and full name? +

We notify waitlist members directly via SMS or WhatsApp as onboarding begins in Lahore. This ensures a verified, bot-free community of genuine buyers and sellers. Email is completely optional for anyone who prefers email confirmations.

What items and designs can be listed on Grabsty? +

Grabsty is curated for archival fashion, designer garments, contemporary quiet luxury, and Lahore Architecture Design collections (including spatial objects, architectural prints, and structural design pieces). Mass-market fast fashion, bootlegs, and counterfeit goods are strictly prohibited.

Is there any fee to join the waitlist or list items? +

Joining the waitlist is 100% free. Founding waitlist sellers also receive waived listing fees and lifetime preferred fee schedules during the launch period.
