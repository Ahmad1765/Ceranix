FREQUENTLY ANSWERED.

When does Cohort 01 Private Alpha launch? +

Cohort 01 onboarding commences in rolling batches. Waitlist members are admitted based on registration timestamp and curated wardrobe profiles. You will receive an invitation passport with private authentication credentials directly via email.

How does the Escrow Buyer Protection safeguard purchases? +

When an item is purchased, your funds are secured in Grabsty Escrow. The seller ships with tracked courier transit. Upon receipt, you have a full 72-hour physical inspection window to verify that the piece precisely matches the listing before payment is released.

What apparel and design houses are eligible for Grabsty? +

Grabsty is strictly focused on archival, runway, and contemporary quiet-luxury wardrobes. We welcome curated archival pieces from houses including Maison Margiela, Helmut Lang, Yohji Yamamoto, Raf Simons, Rick Owens, Phoebe Philo era Celine, Jil Sander, and select avant-garde ateliers. Fast fashion and counterfeit goods are strictly prohibited.

Are there platform listing fees for early sellers? +

Cohort 01 Founding Sellers receive lifetime preferred fee schedules and zero listing fees during the Private Alpha period. All sellers undergo strict identity and archival inventory verification prior to publishing.
